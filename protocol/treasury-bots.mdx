## Treasury philosophy

SolMarket uses revenue to make the product feel active, but capital must be protected. The treasury should never behave like an unlimited market maker.

<Info>
  The treasury's job is to create opportunity, not to subsidize every market forever.
</Info>

---

## Revenue streams

| Source | Destination | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Hatch SOL-side fees | Market treasury | Funds operations, reserves, and selective market growth. |
| Hatch token-side fees | Burn | Reduces supply automatically through Dev Mode. |
| Prediction-market fee | Buyback wallet | Funds buyback and burn once the minimum threshold is reached. |
| Recovered seed | Market treasury | Recycled into future market creation. |

---

## Default allocation

Hatch SOL-side fees are split conservatively:

| Bucket | Share | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Market reinvestment | 15% | Create selected markets and seed both sides. |
| Development/profit | 55% | Founder upside, development, and business sustainability. |
| Reserve | 20% | Treasury buffer and volatility protection. |
| Operations | 10% | Infrastructure, monitoring, and ongoing costs. |

Prediction-market fees are simpler: they go to buyback and burn, with execution waiting until the wallet has enough balance to make the burn meaningful.

---

## Seed levels

| Level | Target | Use case |
|---|---:|---|
| Probe | $5 per side | Test market demand. |
| Standard | $10 per side | Normal quality market. |
| Strong | $20 per side | High-confidence opportunity. |
| Premium | $50 per side | Manual, sponsored, or partner market. |

The orchestrator can spend less than the target if the optimizer finds that fewer tickets leave the market cleaner.

---

## When to scale

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Broad user volume across many markets | Increase market count and move top opportunities to Standard or Strong. |
| High token attention but weak market usage | Create fewer markets and keep seed conservative. |
| Volume concentrated in a few markets | Reinforce winners and reduce long-tail experiments. |
| Low activity everywhere | Pause expansion and focus on recovery. |
| Seed recovery deteriorates | Reduce seed tiers and tighten filters. |

---

## Public proof of work

Users should be able to see that capital is active without seeing internal implementation details.

Recommended public metrics:

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Markets created this week | Shows the protocol is alive. |
| Treasury deployed into seed | Shows money working. |
| Capital recovered | Shows discipline. |
| Buyback pending | Shows fee accumulation. |
| Tokens burned | Shows deflation. |
| Holder proposals reviewed | Shows the community is heard. |

This creates a simple story: revenue enters, markets get funded, users trade, fees buy back the token, and burns reduce supply.
