## Overview

SolMarket separates protocol money flows into two wallets:

| Wallet | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| `MARKET_TREASURY_WALLET` | Receives Hatch SOL-side fee claims and funds automated market creation/seed liquidity. |
| `BET_FEE_WALLET` | Receives prediction-market fees and is reserved for buyback and burn. |

<Info>
  The market bot is intentionally conservative. Its job is to create enough high-quality markets to drive volume, not to recycle all protocol revenue into seed bets.
</Info>

---

## Treasury allocation

Every detected Hatch SOL-side fee inflow is recorded and split by policy:

| Allocation | Default share | Purpose |
|------------|---------------|---------|
| Market reinvestment | 15% | Fund selected automated markets and initial YES/NO seed. |
| Developer/profit | 55% | Founder upside, development, and business profit. |
| Reserve | 20% | Safety buffer and future strategic uses. |
| Operations | 10% | Infrastructure, RPC, monitoring, and admin costs. |

```env
TREASURY_MARKET_REINVEST_BPS=1500
TREASURY_DEV_PROFIT_BPS=5500
TREASURY_RESERVE_BPS=2000
TREASURY_OPERATIONS_BPS=1000
```

Example with `5,000 USDC` equivalent revenue:

| Bucket | Amount |
|--------|--------|
| Market reinvestment | `750 USDC` |
| Developer/profit | `2,750 USDC` |
| Reserve | `1,000 USDC` |
| Operations | `500 USDC` |

This avoids the bad outcome where most revenue is automatically spent creating too many markets.

## Market spend caps

The market bot has a second layer of protection. Even inside the market reinvestment bucket, it does not consider the entire treasury spendable.

```env
TREASURY_MAX_MARKET_SPEND_BPS=1000
TREASURY_MARKET_RUNWAY_CAP_USDC=250
MARKET_MIN_TREASURY_USDC=10
MAX_AUTO_MARKETS_PER_HOUR=3
```

Spendable market budget is the smaller of:

1. `10%` of USDC above the minimum reserve
2. `250 USDC` runway cap

So if the treasury has thousands of USDC, the bot still behaves like an operator with a fixed market-making budget, not like an unlimited spender.

## Initial liquidity seed

Automated markets start near `50/50` by buying both sides in tiny balanced chunks.

```env
BOT_SEED_MIN_USDC_PER_SIDE=2
BOT_SEED_SHORT_USDC=2
BOT_SEED_NORMAL_USDC=5
BOT_SEED_PREMIUM_USDC=10
BOT_SEED_MAX_USDC_PER_SIDE=10
```

At a `50/50` start:

| Seed per side | Tickets per side | Total market seed |
|---------------|------------------|-------------------|
| `2 USDC` | 4 YES + 4 NO | `4 USDC` |
| `5 USDC` | 10 YES + 10 NO | `10 USDC` |
| `10 USDC` | 20 YES + 20 NO | `20 USDC` |

The default hard cap is `10 USDC` per side.

---

## Why chunks are small

The contract price changes after each buy. If a bot bought a large YES chunk first and then bought NO, the NO leg could execute at a different price. Ticket counts could end equal, but the USDC pools could be uneven.

To avoid that, SolMarket seeds with fixed chunks:

```text
TX 1: buy YES 2 tickets + buy NO 2 tickets
TX 2: buy YES 2 tickets + buy NO 2 tickets
TX 3: buy YES 2 tickets + buy NO 2 tickets
...
```

Two tickets cost `1 USDC` at `50/50`, satisfying the contract minimum while keeping visible odds and pools aligned.

## Market selection

The market engine scores both the system and the token candidate.

System score includes:

- recent Hatch SOL-side fee claims
- treasury SOL/USDC health
- recent platform volume
- penalty for active markets with no volume

Candidate score includes:

- 5-minute and 1-hour volume
- recent transaction count
- liquidity
- volatility
- market-cap range

The bot creates a market only when the combined score is high enough. If too many active markets have no user volume, the bot enters conservative mode.
